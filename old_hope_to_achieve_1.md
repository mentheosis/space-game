# Hope To Achieve Iteration 1

Iteration 1 should push the prototype shuttle from a structurally readable blockout toward an intentional early vertical-slice interior. The goal is not final art, but each aspect should move by a large enough step that the next render can show a meaningful change in quality rather than a minor tweak.

## 1. Material Families

I want the next render to show clear material separation at a glance: dark structural metal should read differently from lighter wall panels, rubber gaskets, floor plates, glass, fabric, and emissive indicators. This is ambitious enough because the current pass still relies heavily on similar gray surfaces; changing the material palette and roughness/metallic balance should immediately improve realism and readability across every interior view.

## 2. Practical Lighting

I want lighting to feel motivated by visible fixtures instead of broad debug fill. The next render should show directional pools from ramp/cargo/cockpit practicals, visible lamp housings, small indicators, and cockpit glow while keeping the interior readable and not overly bright. This is ambitious because lighting affects the entire spatial read of the ship and will expose whether our modeled fixtures actually explain the illumination.

## 3. Ramp/Cargo Polish

I want the ramp and cargo bay to gain a second layer of believable manufactured detail: scuffed tread surfaces, latch points, side mechanisms, cable trays, equipment panels, and deeper wall/ceiling detail. This is ambitious because it should make the first half of the walkthrough feel like a cargo spacecraft interior rather than a clean room with panels attached.

## 4. Cockpit Readability

I want the two-seat cockpit to become cleaner and more legible: seats should have stronger silhouette and fabric separation, consoles should read as purposeful controls, and canopy/floor framing should lead the eye forward without visual clutter. This is ambitious because the cockpit is where scale and quality are judged most harshly, and it must stop reading as blocky generic geometry.

## 5. Evidence Update

I want the next render check to include both the existing contact sheet and a new material/lighting walkthrough. The walkthrough should begin at the ramp, sweep across the cargo interior, turn into the stair route, and perform a second sweep near the cockpit chair. This is ambitious enough because it will reveal lighting, materials, floor continuity, stair readability, and cockpit legibility in one continuous review artifact instead of hiding problems in isolated screenshots.
