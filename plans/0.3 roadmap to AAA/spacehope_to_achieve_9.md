# Space Hope To Achieve Iteration 9

Iteration 9 should improve player-route readability and final composition. The model has enough detail for this sprint; now the route from ramp to cargo to stairs to cockpit should read more intentionally.

## 1. Material Families

I want route surfaces to use a consistent family: warm worn walk panels, darker rubber edges, brushed metal thresholds, and pale adjacent wall panels.

## 2. Practical Lighting

I want practical lights to reinforce the route: ramp threshold, cargo centerline, stair landings, and cockpit entry should each have a visible cue.

## 3. Ramp/Cargo Polish

I want the loading path to read as designed: add restrained route arrows, threshold bands, and cargo-to-stair transition cues.

## 4. Cockpit Readability

I want the cockpit entry and pilot station to read as the destination of the route. Add entry framing and low guide cues near the seats without obstructing movement or view.

## 5. Evidence Update

I want the moving walkthrough to make the route legible even when the camera sweeps away from straight ahead. Static views should still show ramp, cargo, stairs, landing, and cockpit.
