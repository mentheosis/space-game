# Space Hope To Achieve Iteration 8

Iteration 8 should add manufacturing detail and small-scale surface credibility without disrupting the larger forms that are now working.

## 1. Material Families

I want fasteners, labels, edge strips, and fixture mounts to introduce small-scale contrast between painted panels, bare metal, rubber, and emissive parts.

## 2. Practical Lighting

I want visible lights to have mounts, bezels, and darker backplates so they stop reading as simple glowing rectangles.

## 3. Ramp/Cargo Polish

I want the ramp and cargo bay to show more constructed detail: bolt rows, hinge fasteners, cargo-panel index marks, and service labels.

## 4. Cockpit Readability

I want cockpit displays and controls to gain small but readable manufactured features: bezels, button banks, fastener strips, and seat attachment details.

## 5. Evidence Update

I want the current evidence to make small-scale detail visible at least in static sheets, while the moving walkthrough should still show larger readability.

## Success Assessment Before Iteration 9

Iteration 8 was successful as a restrained manufacturing-detail pass.

The material family target improved through fasteners, button banks, and small label/edge details.

The practical lighting target improved indirectly because fixture backers and mounts now make light panels feel more physically attached.

The ramp/cargo polish target improved with threshold bolts and guide detail.

The cockpit readability target improved only slightly; small details help static evidence more than the moving walkthrough.

The evidence target succeeded but also showed diminishing returns from micro-detail. Iteration 9 should focus on final route/readability cleanup rather than adding many more small pieces.
