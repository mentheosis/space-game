# Space Hope To Achieve Iteration 6

Iteration 6 should improve what the required cockpit side sweep actually sees. The target is not more forward console detail; it is side-cockpit structure, canopy-side surfaces, and light pools that remain readable at 90-degree look angles.

## 1. Material Families

I want side-cockpit surfaces to have clear material separation: pale inner frame, dark rubber seals, brushed metal utility rails, smoked side displays, and fabric/soft trim near the seat shoulders.

## 2. Practical Lighting

I want side-cockpit practical lights to reveal the canopy sill, seat edges, and side consoles during the wide sweep. The light should come from visible small fixtures and displays rather than from ambient fill.

## 3. Ramp/Cargo Polish

I want only continuity polish in the cargo area: subtle side labels or guide details that keep the loading path coherent without stealing focus from the cockpit work.

## 4. Cockpit Readability

I want the cockpit side views to stop reading as black empty masses. This pass should add side cockpit liner panels, canopy-side rails, side display blocks, and seat-shoulder breakup that make left/right sweep frames visibly cockpit-like.

## 5. Evidence Update

I want the moving walkthrough to continue exposing the side sweep, but the static sheet should also retain oblique cockpit views that show whether these side liners and lights are visible.

## Success Assessment Before Iteration 7

Iteration 6 was modestly successful.

The material family target improved on side cockpit surfaces: pale side liners, darker inserts, brushed rails, cyan side displays, and fabric shoulder details now appear in the side sweep.

The practical lighting target improved with visible side-display glow, though the cockpit still has more darkness than ideal.

The ramp/cargo polish target stayed intentionally small and stable.

The cockpit readability target improved slightly: the required side sweep no longer reads only as empty blackness, but the side and overhead structure still lacks enough physical depth.

The evidence target succeeded in showing the side-panel additions. Iteration 7 should now attack broader wall/ceiling flatness and light-source believability across the whole walkthrough.
